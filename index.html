📝 Issue Credit Note
What is a Credit Note?
A Credit Note reduces the value of a previously issued bill/invoice. Use it when:
• Customer returned goods (full or partial)
• You overcharged and want to issue a refund
• Order was cancelled after billing
• Quality issue — partial credit given

The original document stays unchanged. The credit note references it and reduces the outstanding amount.
